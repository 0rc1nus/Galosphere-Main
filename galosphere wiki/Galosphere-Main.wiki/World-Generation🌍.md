## Crystal Canyons
* These can be found all around the world, but it is most commonly found in cold biomes
* Contains massive crystals and calcite patches

## Lichen Caves
* This cave biome can be found in any biomes. But they generate in bigger chunks if it is in a humid area. 
* Contains lichen moss patches
* Contains large lichen mushrooms
* Contains tall lichen cordyceps

## Pink Salt Caves
* Usually found in mountainous areas
* Covered in pink salt patches that are placed in a noise map
* Covered in pink salt straw clusters
* Contains mini pink salt pools
* Contains a "Pink Salt Shrine" Structure

## Pink Salt Shrine
* A new underground structure that spawns in Pink Salt Caves
* It contains lucrative loot and ominous rooms
* It also contains a mysterious statue at the end of the structure which could potentially lead to a phenomenally challenging battle 

## Forgotten Ruins
* A new barren structure infested with silverfish that spawns anywhere underground
* It contains infested structure pieces with suspicious gravel
* It is also the only place where you can find mole burrows 

## Natural Generation
* Palladium ores can be found at y level -63 to 8
* Palladium Dust Veins can generate next to stones that cover ores
