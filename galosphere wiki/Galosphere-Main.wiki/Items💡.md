## Palladium
* Items: Palladium Ingot, Raw Palladium, Palladium Nugget
## Crystal shards
* Items: Allurite shard, Lumiere Shard
## Mining Pouch
* Mining Pouches are found in mole burrows after moles buried them
* Mining Pouches introduces a new system called "Mining Quests"
* Mining Pouches have a list of requested items in their tooltips which a player needs to collect the respective items and put them inside the pouch
* Once inserted, it cannot be taken out again
* When the Pouch is fulfilled, it can be returned to any mole burrow and the mole burrow will start spitting gifts out

## Pottery Sherds
* Items: Thirst, Wave (Found in Forgotten Ruins Suspicious blocks)
## Ancient City Map
* Can be found through mining quests
* Directs to the nearest ancient city on a map
## Faith Armor Trim
* Can be found through mining quests
## Opal Flakes
* Can be found through mining quests
* When used on any saplings, it will convert them into opal saplings which can be grown into an opal tree
## Broken Echo Bell
* Can be found through mining quests
## Echo Bell
* Echo bell is a trinket that can be crafted with Broken Echo bell, 2 echo shards, 1 allurite shard, and a stick
* It needs to be fueled with allurite shard and toggled in order to be activated
* Echo Bell will emit crystal sounds or particles occasionally when it detects an ore (5x3x5)
* The particles will direct you to the ore position
* The fuel depletes every minute
## Rope Dart
* Rope Dart can be crafted with an Allurite Shard and a String. When thrown, a player must continue holding until it can attaches something. The rope will automatically pull the player to the attached direction similar to a grappling hook. However, player's horizontal inputs can affect the pulling movement
## Monstrometer
* Monstrometer is a Palladium Trinket that can detect Monster areas by emitting blue or yellow indicator particles. 
* Monstrometer needs to be fueled with lumiere shard and toggled in order to be activated
* Blue Particle: Monsters can spawn
* Yellow Particle: Monsters cannot spawn
* The fuel depletes after every use
## Palladium Bomb
* This projectile can be customized with combustion table to enhance its traits. As expected, they will explode after a certain amount of time. They can only explode blocks that have a low resistance
## Sterling Armor
* Items: Sterling helmet, Sterling chestplate, Sterling leggings, Sterling boots, Sterling Horse Armor
* These armors have a new attribute named Illager Resistance which mitigates damage from illagers. In addition, they will prevent the players from sinking into the powder snow
* Putting a banner to a helmet in inventory attaches the banner to the helmet (Similar to how you put items inside a bundle)
## Bottle of Spectre
* Bottle of Spectre can be obtained by capturing a spectre with a glass bottle
## Lichen Cordyceps
* Items: Lichen Cordyceps, Golden Lichen Cordyceps
* Lichen cordyceps is a new type of food source that gives 2 food points and 0.2 saturation ratio. Apart from giving saturation, it would also increase the player's air supply by one bubble. You can also make golden lichen cordyceps which can be crafted with gold nuggets. Golden lichen cordyceps would increase the player's air supply by 3 bubbles. In addition, consuming the golden lichen cordyceps at max air supply would allow the players to gain extra air supply
## Glow Flare
* Glow flare is a throwable rocket projectile that can be launched by hand or crossbow. When the glow flare hits a block, it will start to spread a new type of light source called glow ink clumps. However, the glow ink clumps will not drop itself after thrown
## Spectre Flare
* Spectre Flare is a throwable rocket projectile that can be launched by hand or crossbow. When the spectre flare hits a block, it will set the player's perspective to the position's perspective, allowing players to see the area 
## Spectre Bound Spyglass
* Spectre Bound Spyglass is a new type of spyglass that can be used to spectate a spectre. The spectating only works if you are close to the linked spectre by 110 blocks
## Cured Membrane
* A new item that can be used to craft a storage block, shadow frames, stranded membranes, and a new potion called Potion of Astral
## Salted Jerky
* A new food source that can be crafted with 4 pink salt shards and 1 rotten flesh
## Preserved Flesh
* A new food source that can be obtained from killing the berserker. This food source is different compared to other food sources, because it has a durability of 180 which only depletes when a player eats it
## Preserved Template
* A new item that can be used in a smithing table with any non-block or shulker box item 
* Whenever a player dies, it will retain the item in the player's inventory
## Saltbound Tablet
* Saltbound Tablet can be obtained by combining a pink salt shard, preserved flesh, and preserved template together in a smithing table
* You can charge the item to create a row of pink salt pillars. If you look down while you're charging it, players can create a circle of pillars around themselves
* Saltbound Tablet can also be enchanted with several vanilla enchantments or new enchantments that can only be found in the pink salt shrine (i.e. Enfeelble, Sustain, and Rupture)
