## Pink Salt Straw
* Block: Pink Salt Straw
* Has a random chance of dropping itself when mined
* When it hangs down from the ceiling, it will check for composters or copper blocks that are in a vertical range of 7. 
* If there is a filled composter underneath it, it will have a 50% chance of converting the composter into a saline composter (Converts the compost into coarse dirt)
* If there is a copper block underneath it, it will have a 0.15% chance of aging the copper.
* 4 Pink Salt Straws can craft a pink salt block
* Pink Salt Straws can also be renewed through this contraption
