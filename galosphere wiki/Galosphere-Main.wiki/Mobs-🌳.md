## Sparkles
* Sparkles are semi-aquatic crystalized lizards that can be found in Crystal Canyons
* They come in 3 variants. (i.e. allurite, lumiere, none)
* They can be bred with glow lichen
* If a sparkle finds a crystal cluster that is not emitting particles / pollinated, they will walk towards it and bite the crystal to form a shattered variant which will start emitting particles. (The texture will not change but it will start emitting particles)
* Right-clicking them with a pickaxe will extract the crystal from the tail
* Fortune enchantment can increase the number of crystals that it drops. This will also take 5-10 minutes for crystal regrowth
* Silk touch enchantment will drop a cluster instead of shards. This will take 10-20 minutes for crystal regrowth
* They will grow a different type of crystal if the extraction has taken place, the regrowth cooldown can be sped up by feeding them with glow lichen

## Spectre
* Spectres are new flying mobs that can be found in Lichen Caves
* They can be bred with lichen shelves which will produce 1-4 specterpillars
* They spawn as an ambient mob. So if you want them to be persistent, you need to collect them with a glass bottle and give them a custom name.
* In order to spectate them, you need to give it with an allurite shard to make them "spectatable", and link it with a spyglass to get a spectre bound spyglass which can be used to spectate the spectres

## Specterpillar
* Specterpillar is a new type of offspring that originates from the spectres. These small adorable creatures can grow into a spectre by feeding them a specific amount of lichen shelves. After they stop eating the shelves, they will find the nearest lichen moss and dig into it and plant a lichen cordyceps onto the moss 

## Preserved
* Preserved is a new zombie variant that is always summoned by a causal factor. They can get spawned by Berserker (Mob/Boss). 
* Preserved is slightly stronger and faster when compared to a regular zombie. Additionally, they will survive in daylight, meaning that they will not conflagrate when exposed to sunlight. 

## Mole
* Mole is a new animal that likes to dig in rocks and soil, and they occasionally like to chill in their mole burrows
* Mole can be bred with spider eyes 
* Mole will make a mole burrow if they are fed with silverfish
* Moles will target silverfish
* Moles will avoid flowers like allium
* When a mole returns to their burrows, they will store a pouch in the mole burrows which can be collected by hand
