## Saltbound Tablet Enchantments
* Sustain: Increases the life time of a pink salt pillar
* Enfeeble: Makes pink salt pillar inflict slowness
* Rupture: Makes pink salt pillar shatter into tiny pieces of shards when it retracts

## Brush Enchantments
* Sifting: (Lvl 1-3) - Increases the chance of producing another suspicious block after its been brushed
