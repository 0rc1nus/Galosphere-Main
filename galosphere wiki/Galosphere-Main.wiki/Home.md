Galosphere aims to expand the underground and the combat system. Currently, it adds one new biome which is the Crystal Canyons with a new type of mineral named silver which can be utilized to craft different objects. 

Team members:
* Owner | Gameplay Developer: Orcinus
* Sound Engineer | Gameplay Developer: bunten
